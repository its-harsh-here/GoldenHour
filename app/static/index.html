The problem

The rule says one hour. Patients wait a day.

Hospitals send a pre-authorization request; a TPA desk reads notes, checks the policy, computes deductions and drafts letters by hand. Discharges stall while the clock runs.

1hour

Insurers must decide on a cashless authorization request within one hour.

IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance, May 2024
3hours

Final discharge authorization must follow within three hours of the hospital's request.

IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance, May 2024
6–48hours

About six in ten claimants reported waiting 6 to 48 hours for claim approval and discharge.

LocalCircles survey, January 2025
How it works

Six agents, one fixed policy, a human at the gate.

Each agent has its own prompt and an allow-list of tools. The rule engine does every rupee of math. The orchestrator can recommend, query or escalate, never deny.

Intake

Redacts PHI, screens every document for prompt injection, extracts a structured case with a confidence score.

Policy

Calls get_policy and get_claim_history; nine pure rules return the eligible amount with clause-cited deductions.

Docs & Coding

Maps ICD-10 to the package table, lists missing documents, measures cost deviation from benchmark.

Risk

Weighted rule signals give a 0–100 score; the model only writes the rationale from those signals.

Orchestrator

Applies pack.yaml: approve with sign-off, query the hospital, or escalate to a Medical Officer.

Human approval

Role-gated: one-click sign-off for clean cases, a Medical Officer with a written reason for anything else.

Action

Letters, hospital queries, patient SMS and status updates, all through the RBAC-checked Action Gateway.

Reporter

SLA and ROI dashboard, per-case decision card and a tamper-evident audit trail.

Guardrails

Eight controls, each one visible in the console.

Responsible AI is not a slide. Every control below can be seen working in the demo.

PHI redaction

Names, phones, Aadhaar-like numbers, emails and policy numbers become tokens before any model call; rehydrated locally only in final letters.

core/redaction.py · "What the model saw"

Injection screening

A guard model plus a regex fallback on every document; offending text is quarantined, highlighted and the case escalates.

core/guard.py · quarantine banner

Explainability

A rule ledger with pass/fail, rupee impact and clause references; summaries citing unsupported figures are rejected.

rules.py · decision card

Role-based access

Processor, Medical Officer, Auditor, Admin. The gateway answers 403 to anything else and logs the attempt.

core/rbac.py · action_gateway.py

Tamper-evident audit

hash = sha256(prev_hash + payload). Verify the chain, or run the Admin tamper test and watch it break at one block.

core/audit.py · Audit tab

Human override

People can change the amount or the decision. Overrides are logged and reported as an override-rate metric.

Dashboard · override rate

Graceful degradation

An LLM error, timeout or 429 routes the case to the human queue with the reason. Nothing fails silently.

core/orchestrator.py

Kill switch

An Admin can force autonomy level 1 instantly; a banner shows it across the console.

pack.yaml autonomy_level · header
